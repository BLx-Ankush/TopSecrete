Critical Anti-Benchmark

The Hard Truth:
Where ALICE is Worse

We built ALICE to be a powerful, open, and unrestricted AI agent. But the architectural choices that give her freedom also introduce fundamental flaws when compared to deeply-embedded, multi-billion-dollar commercial operating systems.

👁️
No Passive Context Awareness

ALICE relies on active querying. She only sees what is currently on the screen when explicitly asked, and only remembers what is explicitly spoken during a LiveKit session.

Copilot
Windows Recall takes passive snapshots of the screen 24/7. You can ask Copilot "what was that blue shoe I looked at yesterday?" and it instantly searches its passive visual database.
ALICE
Because ALICE is a Python script, running constant 24/7 screen recording would destroy local performance. She is completely blind to anything that happens outside of an active query.
🧱
Overlay Fragility vs Deep Embedding

ALICE operates purely as a visual overlay using `pyautogui` and Gemini Flash. She looks at the screen like a human does, which is great for legacy apps, but highly prone to breaking.

Apple
Apple Intelligence uses App Intents to talk directly to the underlying code of an app. If a button is hidden under a menu, Siri can still trigger its code programmatically instantly.
ALICE
If a pop-up blocks the screen, or if a button is scrolled out of view, ALICE fails. She literally has to click "scroll down" and look again, mimicking human friction instead of bypassing it.
🔋
Power Drain & Hardware Demands

To achieve low latency and local fallback, ALICE requires significant compute power. A local VLM or LLM fallback will drain a laptop battery incredibly fast.

Apple/MS
Commercial OS AIs route tasks to dedicated low-power NPUs (Neural Processing Units). They run background SLMs using almost zero battery.
ALICE
Rerouting to Ollama spins up the main GPU/CPU. When ALICE uses her vision engine, taking a screenshot, hashing it, and sending it over HTTPS is incredibly inefficient compared to on-device tensor execution.
🛡️
The Prompt Injection Attack Surface

While ALICE now has a "Conversational Manifest", she fundamentally runs inside a user-space Python process with access to the terminal and filesystem.

iOS / macOS
Apple Intelligence operates in a heavily sandboxed ring. Even if an attacker uses prompt injection to say "Delete everything", Siri simply does not possess the kernel-level authority to wipe a drive.
ALICE
ALICE uses LLM decision-making to control `subprocess.Popen`. A sophisticated prompt injection hidden in a webpage ALICE is reading could theoretically trick her into bypassing her own Python guardrails.
📱
Ecosystem Isolation

ALICE is a hyper-localized agent. She lives strictly on the machine where her Python environment is running.

Gemini
Gemini Advanced syncs seamlessly. You ask it to draft an email on your phone, and it's instantly available in Google Docs on your desktop, with context of your Maps data.
ALICE
ALICE has no cloud-sync companion app. She cannot access your phone's GPS, she cannot text you when you are away from the computer, and her Mem0 database is stranded locally.
